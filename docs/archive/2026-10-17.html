Read the gauge, not just the table.

The tide table says 3.4 ft at 6:42a, but the La Jolla gauge read 4.6. Warm water and the swell itself can hold the sea a foot above the prediction for days. Add today’s gap to the table and Little Point fills to its ceiling around 1p instead of never. Check the live gauge before you trust the printed tide.

Gotchas

Little Point is a shallow reef with a tight takeoff, so it crowds fast on a Saturday, and the rocks show as it drains. High rip current risk today (NWS SRF). Fourteen-second sets mean lulls, then a solid set. Hot again: the pier read 81°F air at 6a and NWS has the coast near 85°F with a slight chance of a stray shower, so bring water for the stairs at Fern Glen. PZZ740 has E 10–15 kt gusting 20 offshore; the beach should be lighter, but do not wait for midday.
