ʻIke · one thing to remember

A tide gauge reading above the table is the ocean standing taller.

Tide tables only know the moon and sun. Warm water expands, and low pressure lets the sea bulge up, so this week the La Jolla gauge is reading about a foot and a half above the prediction. On a reef with a tide ceiling, that pushes the good window later on a morning high, so trust the gauge over the table.
