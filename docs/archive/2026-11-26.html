ʻIke · one thing to remember

Why 8 feet at 15 seconds is not just twice 4 feet.

Wave energy grows with height squared times period. Today's 7.9 ft at 15s carries about four times the energy of Friday's forecast 4 ft at 14s, so it shoals taller, breaks harder, and pulls harder in the rips. Long period also means long lulls: count sets for ten minutes before you judge the size.

Gotchas

High Surf Advisory through 4a Friday, HIGH rip risk, and a Coastal Flood Warning through 1p Friday. The beach will be narrow with runup around the 9:15a high, so stay off the seawall and the pier stairs. 15 to 18 second sets come in long, heavy bunches after long lulls: a quiet 10 minutes is not a smaller day. If you go out at Tourmaline, stay inside, keep the rocks to the north in mind, and if a set catches you, go under and let the rip carry you out before you paddle sideways.
