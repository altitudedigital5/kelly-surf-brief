Watch the gauge, not just the table.

Tide tables only predict the pull of the moon and sun. Warm water, low pressure, and swell can lift the real sea level above that, and the La Jolla gauge has run close to a foot high all week. On a shallow reef with a tide ceiling, that foot moves your window up by about 40 minutes.

Skip

Little Point after about 9:15a, when the high tide goes over its ceiling, and again in the afternoon as it drains under N wind. Everything from 9:30a to 2p while it is flooded. Calumet, Tourmo, and PB Point on this high. The Cliffs and OB on a SSW swell.

Gotchas

Little Point is a shallow reef with a tight takeoff, so it crowds fast and the rocks show as it drains. High rip current risk and a Beach Hazards Statement through 2p today (NWS). Sets to 6 ft at 15 seconds mean long lulls, then a big set. A Heat Advisory runs 10a today through 8p Saturday, so bring water for the stairs at Fern Glen.
