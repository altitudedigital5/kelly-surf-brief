ʻIke · one thing to remember

Why a full tide softens a long-period swell.

A 14 second swell carries a lot of energy deep, so when it hits a shallow sandbar it stands up fast and pitches. With more water over the bar (today the tide is about a foot higher than the table), the wave feels the bottom later and breaks with a rounder, softer face. As the tide drains toward the 10:44a low, the same swell gets steeper and hollower. That is why the early, fuller tide is your window.

Skip

PB Drive: long-period WNW sets on a draining tide close out fast and hollow, beyond the fish. Little Point: falling tide until 10:44a. PB Point and Birdrock: tide too high early. Blacks: too thin at 46225 for the drive.

Gotchas

HIGH rip current risk. NWS says tides are running 1.2 to 1.4 ft over predictions, so expect strong runup and backwash at the stairs. Sets are at the top of your range: wait them out on the shoulder, and do not paddle across the impact zone. Recent rain keeps the county 72-hour runoff rule on: stay away from river mouths and storm drains.
