Why 17 seconds hits harder than 3 feet sounds.

Wave energy grows with height squared times period, and long-period waves feel the bottom from much deeper water. A 3 ft swell at 17s shoals up taller and steeper than 3 ft at 10s: reefs get punchy sets after long lulls, and flat sandbars tend to close out.

Skip

Little Point: falling all morning and only refills after dark. PB Drive and PB north: 17s lines close out on the sandbar. PB Point: the swell is too south. Anything before 9:30: swamped at about 7.5 ft real. After 2p: minus low, then N to NW wind.

Gotchas

Coastal Flood Advisory until 4a Tuesday, then a Coastal Flood Warning Tuesday 4a to Friday 1p: narrow, backwashy beach around the high. HIGH rip risk today. Calumet: shallow reef that gets rocky as the tide drops past 2 ft real, so get out by about 12:30; entry is down the Calumet Park stairs. Blacks: steep, loose gliderport trail (about 20 min down, 25 up), heavy shorebreak and rips at the main peaks; stay out from under the cliffs. Long-period sets come in bunches after long lulls, so count before you paddle out.
