ʻIke · one thing to remember

Why a flood high plus big surf is extra dangerous.

The La Jolla gauge is running about 1.4 ft above the tide table because of the storm (low pressure lifts the sea and onshore wind piles water against the coast). Add that to a 4.9 ft high and big sets, and wave runup reaches much farther up the beach than normal. That is why NWS has a Coastal Flood Advisory today: the backwash gets strong, the sand gets narrow, and getting in and out through the shorebreak is the hardest part of the session.

Size: SRF 6 to 8 ft with sets to 10 south of Del Mar, and the buoy has about 5 ft of 13–14s W groundswell plus 6 ft of 10s windswell (Hs 7.5 ft), so this is well past your ceiling on the Anvil. Friday and Saturday rain keeps the county 72-hour runoff rule on, so stay away from river mouths and storm drains.
