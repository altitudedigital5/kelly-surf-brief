ʻIke · one thing to remember

The land breathes in at noon.

Dawn is glassy because land and sea sit near the same temperature. On a heat-warning day the mesas bake fast, hot air rises, and cool ocean air rushes in to replace it. That is the NW sea breeze, and today NWS has it arriving by 10 to noon. Surf before the land wakes up.
