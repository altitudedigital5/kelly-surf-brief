Why sets to 7 can still hide a fun medium wave.

Surf Height in the NWS report is the range of most waves, and sets are the bigger waves that show every several minutes. On a 12 second swell mixed with a 9 second windsea, the bigger waves arrive in groups with lulls between. Sitting a little inside of the main peak lets you pick off the medium waves in the lulls and duck the set waves.

Skip

The swampy early high tide everywhere. Little Point (drowned early, falling later). Open-beach Drive closeouts on the bigger sets. Blacks without a thruster (overhead, beyond the fish). The drained, windy low after about 2p.

Gotchas

Size: SRF 4 to 6 ft with sets to 7 on mixed 290° and 210° swell, which is above your comfort zone on the sets; the bigger open-beach waves are beyond the fish on the Anvil. Sit inside, take the medium ones, and give the set waves room. HIGH rip current risk plus a Beach Hazards Statement through 10p Tue (NWS SRF). King-tide pattern: the La Jolla gauge is running about 1.3 ft over predictions with a coastal flood advisory through noon Tuesday, so the beach is narrow and backwashy near the high and the drain to a −0.5 low is fast. Shorter 12s period plus a 9s windsea makes it a bit more mixed and shifty than yesterday. Blacks: hike, shorebreak, canyon crowds, thruster territory.
