Why the tide still runs high.

The tide table only knows the moon and sun. Real water also rises with warm water, low air pressure and onshore setup, and right now La Jolla is reading over a foot above the table. Add the table highs near 6 ft and the real morning highs land past 7 ft, which swamps the sandbars. So time sessions off the live gauge, and wait for the drop.

Skip

The swampy early high tide everywhere. Little Point (drowned early, falling later). Open-beach Drive closeouts on the long-period S pulses. Everything once NW 10 fills in around 1p.

Gotchas

Size: SRF 2 to 4 ft with sets to 5 on mixed 280° and 200° swell — in your soft waist-to-shoulder zone, but sit wide for the longer S pulses. King-tide pattern: the La Jolla gauge is running 1.3 to 1.6 ft over predictions and NWS has a coastal flood advisory through noon Tuesday, so the beach is narrow and backwashy near the high. Moderate rip current risk today (NWS SRF); HIGH rips Saturday. Blacks: hike from the gliderport, shorebreak, rips, and canyon crowds. Tourmaline: packed with longboards, watch for loose boards.
