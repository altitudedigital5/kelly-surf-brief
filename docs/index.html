ʻIke · one thing to remember

Long period means patience.

At 15 to 17 seconds the swell travels in tight groups with long gaps between them. The buoy says only 2 ft, but each set carries more energy than the number suggests and shows up bigger on the sandbar. Count the sets from the beach before you paddle, and sit where the last one broke rather than chasing the lull.

Skip

Little Point today: drowned by the gauge-adjusted high until about 2p, then onshore SW 10 mph as it drains. Calumet and PB Point on a tide this full. The Cliffs and OB on 2 ft of groundswell. Anything after noon, once the S to SW wind fills.

Gotchas

The 15 to 17 second sets come in small groups with long lulls, so be patient.
