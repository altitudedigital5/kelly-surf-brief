ʻIke · one thing to remember

Long period wants room to draw up.

A 20-second groundswell organizes from way outside, but on a flood high the bars are buried and the wave dumps late or closes out. Waiting an hour or two after the high lets the sandbars come back into play so that period energy can actually stand up instead of jacking and dying.
