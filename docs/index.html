A lower high buys you time.

Yesterday the table high was 5.1 ft and Little Point topped out near 9:15a. Today the high is only 4.7 ft at noon, so even with the gauge still running about a foot over the table, the reef stays in its 3.0–5.5 ft band almost an hour longer. Same anomaly, slower climb — that is free morning.

Skip

Little Point after about 10:30a, when the tide anomaly crosses the 5.5 ft ceiling and NE gusts fill. Everything from late morning through the noon high. Calumet, Tourmo, and PB Point on this flood. The Cliffs and OB on a SSW primary swell.

Gotchas

Little Point is a shallow reef with a tight takeoff, so it crowds fast and the rocks show as it drains. High rip current risk today (NWS SRF). Sets at 15 seconds mean long lulls, then a solid set. Heat is back — NWS grid tops near 89°F with NE wind, so bring water for the stairs at Fern Glen. PZZ740 has NE 10–15 kt with gusts to 20 in the marine zone; the beach may stay lighter early, but do not wait for midday.
