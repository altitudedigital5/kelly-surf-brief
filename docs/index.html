Why the surf grows after the forecast says it will.

Long-period swell runs ahead of the storm that made it. The first long waves (forerunners) show up as a few tall, slow sets with long lulls, often hours before the buoy shows the full size. When the advisory says the swell builds in the afternoon, treat the late morning as the last calm window.

Skip

Blacks: overhead canyon closeouts, thruster needed. Little Point: drowned, then falling to a minus low, with no fill before sunset. PB Point and Calumet: too small or blocked until the W fills. Anything before 10:30: swamped at about 7 to 8 ft real. After 1p: High Surf Advisory, NW wind.

Gotchas

HIGH rip risk all day, and a High Surf Advisory from 1p today to 4a Friday. Coastal Flood Warning until 1p Friday: the beach is narrow and backwashy around the high, so watch the stairs and seawall at the pier. A building 14s W swell sends sets in bunches after long lulls. Count a few before you paddle, and if a set clears the lineup, call it a day. Stay off the pier pilings with the strong drift.
