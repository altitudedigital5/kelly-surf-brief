Why a fast-draining king tide changes the wave every hour.

Today the tide drops almost seven feet in seven hours, from a 7:42a high to a −0.4 low. The same swell breaks fat and backwashy at the top, then lines up and gets steeper as the sandbars come shallow. Surf the middle of that drop, and expect the waves to get punchier the later you stay.

Skip

The swampy early high tide everywhere. Little Point (drowned early, falling later). Open-beach Drive closeouts on the long W sets. Blacks without a thruster (beyond the fish). The drained very low tide after about 2p.

Size: SRF 3 to 6 ft on mixed 280° and 220° swell — near your ceiling; the bigger open-beach sets are beyond the fish on the Anvil. Sit inside and give the longer pulses room. HIGH rip current risk plus a Beach Hazards Statement through Tue (NWS SRF). King-tide pattern: the La Jolla gauge is running about 1.1 ft over predictions with a coastal flood advisory through noon Tuesday, so the beach is narrow and backwashy near the high, and the drain to a −0.4 low is fast.
