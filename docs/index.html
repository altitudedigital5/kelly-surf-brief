Why Blacks runs bigger than PB.

Scripps Canyon cuts deep water right up to Blacks. Waves over the canyon keep their speed while waves on the shallow shelf beside it slow down, so the crests bend toward the edges of the canyon and stack up there. Same swell, a size bigger on the peaks.

Skip

Little Point: falling through its window all morning and only back to 3 ft real around sunset. PB Point and Calumet: the W swell is too small for them. Anything before 9: swampy at about 7 ft real. Afternoon: very low tide and showers possible.

Gotchas

Coastal Flood Advisory 4a Sunday to 4a Tuesday: the beach is narrow and backwashy around the high. Moderate rips today, HIGH on Monday. Blacks: steep, loose gliderport trail (budget about 20 min down and 25 up), crowded Sunday lineup, heavy shorebreak and rips at the main peaks; stay out from under the cliffs. E wind gusting to 15 at the pier early: paddling out is easy, but it holds the lips up, so takeoffs get a bit steeper.
