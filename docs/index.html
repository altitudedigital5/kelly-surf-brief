ʻIke · one thing to remember

Long period hits harder per foot.

A 15 second swell is moving deep water, so it feels the bottom sooner, wraps in, and stands up taller on the bar than the same buoy height at 9 seconds. That is why 5.9 ft at 15s reads overhead on the beach, and why the sets come in long lulls then bunches.
