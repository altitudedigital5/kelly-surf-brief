Long period, long lulls.

Seventeen-second swell travels in groups. The buoy jumped from 3.3 to 4.9 ft between readings overnight because it caught a set, not because the swell doubled. At the reef that means five quiet minutes and then three waves that are bigger than they looked from the stairs. Sit a full set cycle before you paddle out, and pick your spot on the set, not the lull.
