ʻIke · one thing to remember

A longer period means more of the wave is hiding below the surface.

A 16 second swell carries energy deep in the water, so it feels the reef sooner and stands up bigger than the buoy height suggests. The sets also arrive in tight groups with long flat lulls. Count the waves in a set, wait out the lull, and be in position before the next group shows.

Skip

Little Point before about 10:45a, while the gauge-adjusted tide sits over the 5.5 ft ceiling, and after about 12:30p, when it drains below the 3.0 ft floor. Calumet, Tourmo and PB Point on a gauge this full. The Cliffs and OB on a short-period W windswell. Any drive north, since home is just as clean.

Gotchas

Moderate rip current risk (NWS SRF). Long 15–17 second sets come in groups with long lulls, so watch a full set cycle before paddling out. The reef drains fast after noon; do not get caught on the inside at 12:30. A coastal flood warning starts 5a Sunday for the big morning highs, so expect beach access and the PB boardwalk to get wet through Thursday. If yesterday s showers hit the coast, the county advises staying out for about 72 hours near storm drains and creek mouths.
